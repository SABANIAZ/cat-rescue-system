About Our Rescue Mission

Our Story

Saba Niaz and Ayesha Javad founded Cat Rescue with a simple but powerful mission: to rescue abandoned and vulnerable cats, provide them with medical care and love, and find them permanent, caring homes.

What started as a small initiative has grown into a recognized rescue organization. We believe every cat deserves a second chance, and we're committed to making that happen through careful rescue, rehabilitation, and adoption support.

🐾

Rescue

We identify and rescue cats in distress, providing immediate safety, shelter, and care.

Rehabilitation

Medical treatment, nutrition, and love help each cat recover and thrive.

Adoption

Careful matching and long-term follow-up ensure every adoption succeeds.
